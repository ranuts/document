Seven known seven-language summary cases were paired current/candidate once each in fixed order on actual default Qwen3-1.7B WebGPU. Candidate prepends instructions to delete irrelevant material and retain exact relevant source phrases, joining them into one shorter sentence. Only this user prefix differs in captured complete Worker inputs; original task data, system prompt, temperature 0, schema, 512-token limit and guards remain unchanged. Exact title and actual Worker model IDs identify the model. This changes summarization strategy/wording, not solely one language variable, and is not heldout acceptance.

Both variants have five native edits and two refusals. Exact native Undo/Redo for edits, original source preserved for refusals, zero previews, unchanged served bundle and no harness errors. Browser closed; no Save. Mechanics are not semantic scores.

All candidate outputs are English, despite six non-English sources. Chinese loses unauthorized-shipping/inspection state and includes excluded parking meeting; refused as not shorter. English retains core facts but remains two sentences. Japanese drops amount/value, inspection pending and current unauthorized state, adding approval conditional wording; applied. Korean drops explicit inspection-pass prerequisite; refused as not shorter. German drops amount/value; applied. Spanish and Portuguese preserve many core facts but remain English and two sentences. Candidate never establishes exact source-phrase retention or reliable language fidelity.

Do not adopt. This experiment weakens an already faithful Chinese baseline and does not meet the original summary requirements. It does not justify changing product summaries into a narrower extraction-only feature. Future improvements must retain the requested facts and language while actually satisfying concise summary/style instructions.

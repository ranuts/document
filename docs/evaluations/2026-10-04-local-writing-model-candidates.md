# Local writing model candidates: catalog and provenance check

Installed patched WebLLM 0.2.85 prebuilt catalog was read directly. Official MLC config files were fetched for three entries and all returned HTTP 200: Llama-3.2-1B-Instruct q4f16_1, gemma3-1b-it q4f16_1, and Ministral-3-3B-Instruct-2512-BF16 q4f16_1. The raw artifact retains URLs, fetched config JSON, response hashes and SDK catalog records. No model weights were fetched and no browser inference was performed.

Catalog estimates are respectively 879.04, 711.07 and 2863.69 MB of required VRAM, with catalog context override 4096. These are SDK estimates, not measured GPU/RAM/download budgets. A catalog entry and available config are prerequisites, not proof of tokenizer/WASM compatibility, load success, offline support or writing quality.

Ministral is the next distinct instruction model worth testing, rather than another edit of the failed Qwen prompt. Its [official model card](https://huggingface.co/mistralai/Ministral-3-3B-Instruct-2512) reports support for Chinese/Japanese/Korean and the European languages in this project's seven-language scope, and JSON/function calling capability. These are publisher claims, not local acceptance. The MLC BF16-derived quantized text-runtime artifact differs from the publisher's FP8 multimodal release; do not infer image capability or identical benchmark scores from its catalog name.

Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507 is a different model from the older Qwen3-4B already evaluated. Its [official model card](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507) reports improved writing/instruction benchmarks and non-thinking-only operation. However, the exact q4f16_1 MLC ID checked is absent from the installed prebuilt catalog. This does not establish that no compatible artifact exists anywhere; using it requires separate trusted conversion/runtime verification. Do not silently substitute the older model or an unverified community conversion.

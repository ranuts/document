Browser HTTP caching is also not separately excluded. There is no model load, inference, native-editor readiness assertion, physical network removal, Safari/iOS, or process restart in this shell-only probe. Do not promote this into full offline acceptance or discard the original failures. No product workaround is justified by the current evidence.

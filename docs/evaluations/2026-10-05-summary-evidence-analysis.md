The [FASum research](https://aclanthology.org/2021.naacl-main.58/) motivates examining factual relations, but uses trained graph attention. This experiment is a different prompt-only mechanism on a small model and neither reproduces nor invalidates that research. Lexical source checks are useful for rejecting nonquotations; they do not establish evidence coverage or final entailment. Further work must address relational fidelity rather than adopt this extra inference stage on these results. Broad local-AI acceptance remains incomplete.

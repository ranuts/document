## Enable AI assistant

Off by default · This browser only. Choose how to use the assistant after enabling it. You start the first download and can cancel at any time. Close the panel to continue editing. Turn off the assistant in its settings to stop tasks. Conversations stay in this session by default; explicitly enable saving in history to keep them.

## How to use AI

**Use on this computer** — Text is processed on this device. First-time setup downloads files and may slow your computer; you can cancel.

**Use your own AI service** — Text you send goes to the selected service. Your own service details are required; service fees may apply.

## How to use it

Select a passage, then choose rewrite, summarize or translate. Task buttons fill a draft and never send automatically.

> Make this paragraph shorter. Keep all dates and numbers.

Compare the original with the result, then confirm to replace the selection. You can also copy it. Use editor Undo if needed; changed selections require a new proposal.

You can stop setup or generation. Failed requests keep your draft. Read-only documents cannot be changed. Offline use depends on files already prepared on the device.

AI may omit or change facts. Check dates, numbers and names before applying. Rewrite, summary and translation currently work on Word selections; other document types support existing explicit operations.
